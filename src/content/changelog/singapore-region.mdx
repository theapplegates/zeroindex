summary: Host projects in Asia Pacific for faster searches across Southeast Asia, Australia and Japan.
---

Projects can now live in Singapore, next to EU West, US East and US West. Searches from Southeast Asia and Australia are typically four to six times faster than from US West.

New projects pick a region when they are created. To move an existing project, create one in Singapore, import your documents and point your app at the new URL. Your keys and settings can be copied over from the dashboard.

## Added

- The Asia Pacific region, at `https://ap1.api.zeroindex.test`.
- Per region response times on the [status page](/status).
